Privacy Policy — Felipe Studio

Last updated: October 29, 2026

What we access: basic profile info (open id, display name, avatar), account statistics (followers, likes, video count) and the list of the connected account's own videos with their view counts. To publish, we upload video files chosen by the account owner.

How we use it: only to publish the owner's videos and to show the owner an internal performance dashboard. We do not sell, share or use this data for advertising.

Storage: access tokens and statistics are kept on the owner's own computer. Nothing is sent to third parties.

Deletion: revoke access in TikTok settings (Security > Manage app permissions) or email us and we delete stored data within 7 days.
